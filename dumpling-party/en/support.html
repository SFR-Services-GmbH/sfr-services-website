Contact

Frequently asked questions

Where is my saved game stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no user account and nothing is stored with us. If you delete the app, your saved game is removed with it, and we cannot restore it. With Apple’s iCloud device backup switched on, your iPhone may back it up too; you control that in the iOS Settings.

I am out of hearts. What now?

You have five hearts, and a lost level costs one. Hearts come back on their own after a waiting time; the app shows you the time until the next heart. If you do not want to wait, you can watch a video for a heart or buy hearts: refill to five, or play unlimited for one hour, four hours or a day.

The next chapter does not open.

A chapter opens once you have won level 25 of the previous one and collected enough stars in it. If you are short, the result card after level 25 tells you how many are missing. You get missing stars by playing levels you have already won again, better: the more moves you have left, the more stars you get, up to three. A level you lose while doing so costs one heart. Chapters you have already opened stay open. Stars are not a currency: you cannot buy them or spend them.

How do I get goodies, and how do I use them?

You get goodies from the daily gift, the weekend party, a chapter finale and purchases. The host’s daily task and a win streak give you Specials that are already on the board in your next level instead. Goodies collect in a stock that counts each kind, with a limit per kind; the stock has its own spot on the map. You place goodies in the level: you pick a goodie and any cell you choose; one goes per level, and using a goodie from the stock costs no move. Swapping the Special you placed counts as a normal move. Every level can also be solved without goodies. You can also buy goodies, as a pack of 3 or of 7 goodies; if there is no room left in your stock for it, the purchase is blocked.

What is the daily gift?

When you first open the game on a day, a wrapped present appears. Tap it, and inside is a goodie. The gift is good until midnight; if you miss a day, nothing happens: no calendar, no streak, no penalty. If there is no room left in your stock for that kind, the gift waits for you until midnight.

What is the weekend party?

From Friday to Sunday the host sets you one big task: collect new stars. Finish it and you get a keepsake for your album and a goodie, once per weekend party. There is no countdown, no leaderboard and no account. The time follows the time on your iPhone. When no weekend party is on, you will not see a badge for it either.

What are the daily task and the win streak?

The host’s daily task is three wins in one day. Once you have done it, a Special is already on the board in your next level. The win streak counts wins in a row on the first try; every third win also gives you a Special in your next level, and a loss resets it. Both are earned and cannot be bought. If you miss a day, nothing happens.

How do I change my consent to ads or analytics?

At any time in the app under Settings: “Allow ads” and “Allow analytics”. The app only shows video ads when you request one yourself. The privacy policy explains what is behind them.

How do I turn off reminders?

In the app under Settings, row “Reminders”, or in the iOS Settings under notifications for Dumpling Party. The app reminds you at most once a day: when your hearts are full again, with a greeting after two days away, and at the start of the weekend party at 10 a.m.

How do I share a snapshot?

After a party scene, a snapshot is in the album. With “Share” you pass it on through the iOS share sheet. The app does not send the picture itself and does not learn who you send it to. If you save it to your photos, iOS asks you for permission.

A purchase did not arrive, or I would like a refund.

Purchases are handled by Apple’s App Store. You request a refund from Apple through your Apple Account. All purchases in the app are consumable purchases (hearts, moves, goodies); so there is no “Restore purchases” function. A purchase the app lost before crediting it is credited at the next start. If paid content still did not arrive in the game, please write to us with the date and time of the purchase.

The game stutters, or the motion makes me uncomfortable.

Under Settings you can turn on “Reduced motion”. Music, effects and haptics can also be adjusted individually there, and “Mute all” turns the sound off completely.
